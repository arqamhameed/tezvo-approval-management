All-in-One Approval Hub for Odoo 19

Centralized Multi-Level Approval Workflow for Sales, Purchase, Accounting, Inventory, and Manufacturing

All-in-One Approval Hub for Odoo 19 centralizes approval requests from Sales, Purchase, Accounting, Inventory, and Manufacturing into one approval workspace. Approvers do not need to open each individual Odoo app to find pending documents. They can review, approve, reject, and track approval requests from one place.

Why This App Is Useful

  • In standard business operations, approvals are often spread across different modules.
  • Users may need to open Sales, Purchase, Accounting, Inventory, or Manufacturing separately to find pending approvals.
  • This app provides one centralized approval hub for cross-module approval management.
  • It reduces module switching and improves approver visibility.
  • It helps managers control pending approvals, approval history, and delegated approvals from one place.

Approval Delegation

Approvers can delegate approval authority to another user for a defined period. This is useful when approvers are on leave or temporarily unavailable.

  • Supports date-range validity.
  • Prevents overlapping delegations.
  • Prevents circular delegation.
  • Delegated approvals maintain approval history and audit trail.

Approval Audit Trail

Submit, approve, reject, cancel, and level transition actions are tracked.

  • Approval history includes user, date, time, level, and action details.
  • Chatter logs help users review approval activity from the related document.

Security and User Rights

Dedicated approval groups are available for controlled access and configuration.

  • Approval User
  • Approval Manager
  • Approval Admin
  • Approval Delegation Officer
  • Approval Back Rules Editor

Approval rights can be managed from the user form. Critical approval configuration records are protected, and only authorized users can amend approval setup and back-rule records.
